Corporate & SECP Compliance

Company incorporation, secretarial services and statutory filings under the Companies Act, 2017.

What it is

Every company registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP) must keep statutory records and file returns on time. Late or missing filings lead to penalties. We handle the paperwork so you can focus on the business.

Who it is for

New businesses incorporating a private limited company, and existing companies that want their statutory records, meetings and SECP filings kept up to date.

What is included

  • Incorporation of private limited and single-member companies
  • Annual return and other statutory filings with the SECP
  • Changes of directors, registered office or share capital
  • Board and general meeting minutes and resolutions
  • Maintenance of statutory registers
  • Compliance calendar with filing reminders
