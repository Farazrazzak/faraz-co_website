About the firm

Faraz Abdul Razzak & Co. is a practice of Chartered Management Accountants in Karachi, established in January 2026 to serve private companies, non-profits and individuals.

Our practice

Finance, governance and compliance under one roof

The firm brings together long experience in audit, accounting, tax and corporate compliance. We work with owner-managed companies, trusts and NGOs, sole proprietors, individuals and associations of persons.

Our work is grounded in the Companies Act 2017, the Income Tax Ordinance 2001, the Sales Tax Act 1990 and the regulations of the SECP, FBR and Sindh Revenue Board.

FR

Mr. Faraz Abdul Razzak, FCMA

Partner & CEO

Faraz Abdul Razzak is the Partner and Chief Executive of Faraz Abdul Razzak & Co., Chartered Management Accountants, Karachi. He is a Fellow member of the Institute of Cost and Management Accountants of Pakistan (ICMAP) and brings 22 years of hands-on experience across financial management, corporate secretarial practice, regulatory compliance and internal audit.

His career has been built largely in Pakistan's insurance industry, a sector with demanding regulatory and reporting standards. At Alpha Insurance he served as Chief Financial Officer, Company Secretary and Acting Head of IT, and earlier as Chief Internal Auditor. Since September 2023 he has served as Company Secretary & Compliance Officer at an insurance company in Karachi.

This mix of finance, governance, audit and technology gives Faraz an end-to-end view of how a business is run and controlled. He works closely with business owners and boards to prepare reliable financial statements, strengthen internal controls, meet SECP and tax obligations, and build systems that support growth.

At the firm, Faraz personally oversees every engagement, so clients get partner-level attention, practical advice and work delivered to professional standards.
